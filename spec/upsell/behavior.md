# Upsell surfaces — spec v1

Incoming spec for cross-sell and upgrade prompts. Not yet distilled into the
knowledge base.

## Rules

| Rule | Statement | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| UPS-001 | An upsell is never shown to an account that already holds the entitlement being upsold. | Proposed |
| UPS-002 | Free-user recommendation threshold: fewer than 4 owned cameras recommends Cam Plus; 4 or more recommends Cam Unlimited. | Proposed |
| UPS-003 | At most 5 upsells may be shown per session. | Proposed |
| UPS-004 | A retail-activated subscription may be upsold to a paid plan; remaining retail term is forfeited at conversion. | Proposed |
