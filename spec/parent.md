## 3. Entitlement data-model rules (ENT)

| ID | Rule | Status | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| ENT-001 | **WSC is the platform-level entitlement layer and reconciliation point.** Never treat Stripe, Apple, or Google alone as the complete entitlement model. | ✅ Confirmed | [WSC as the brain of our apps](https://wyzelabs.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/PMT/pages/4075192341) |
| ENT-002 | **A subscription record ≠ a bound camera.** Parent plans can cover an account while child add-ons carry their own subscription, prerequisite, device, and binding state. | ✅ Confirmed | [PRD: Subscription Add-On System](https://wyzelabs.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/PMT/pages/3384737860) |

| ENT-009 | Add-on **sharing behavior follows the parent plan's sharing rules**. | ✅ Confirmed | [PRD: Subscription Add-On System](https://wyzelabs.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/PMT/pages/3384737860) |
| ENT-010 | Identify plans by canonical `plan_id` mapping, never display text (T3 displays "Cam Unlimited Pro" while carrying `cam-ultimate`). | ✅ Confirmed | [Membership Plan & Service](https://wyzelabs.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/TECH/pages/1789690275/Membership+Plan+Service) |

**Reference — conceptual entitlement shape** (reasoning aid; the [Platform API Product Doc](https://wyzelabs.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/PMT/pages/3842441258) defines the concrete contract):

```text
Account
├── Parent subscription(s): source, plan_id/plan_code, tier, status, dates, logical quantity/coverage
└── Child add-on subscription(s): add_on_id/service code, parent_subscription_uuid,
    prerequisites, device_id (when bound), model eligibility, child status + lifecycle dates
```

Do NOT reintroduce the older fixed-dollar savings or "coverage stays exactly the same" language. | ✅ Confirmed | [CAMPLUSN-946](https://wyzelabs.atlassian.net/browse/CAMPLUSN-946), [BAS-765](https://wyzelabs.atlassian.net/browse/BAS-765) |
